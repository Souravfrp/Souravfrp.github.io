COMPLETED RESEARCH · v1.0.0

Quant AI Market Lab

An eight-ETF research workflow covering validated returns, PCA, market-condition models, SPY risk forecasts, and long-only minimum-variance portfolios. I compare methods chronologically and document backtest timing, benchmarks, costs, and numerical checks.

In the historical portfolio study, minimum-variance approaches had lower realized volatility in the reported subperiods. Equal-weight and SPY benchmarks finished with higher value in the zero-cost full-period comparison. This is retrospective evidence, not a live strategy.

IN PROGRESS · RAW DATA VALIDATED

PaymentGuard

A payment-risk study asking how to balance fraud loss, customer friction, and limited manual-review capacity. The completed stage verifies the data source and profiles 1.09 million raw transactions in memory-controlled chunks. SQL analysis, models, and the approve/review/block policy remain planned work.

RSA Educational Project

My INSPIRE Summer Project implemented RSA key generation, encryption, and decryption for an ASCII message. The repository preserves the 2019 original separately from a tested 2026 revision and explains the number theory and security limitations. It is an educational implementation.
